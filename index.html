About Us - Minenhle's Kitchen

About Us

Our Story

Founded in 2023, Minenhle's Kitchen started in a home kitchen in Durban to share authentic South African meals with the community. Every dish is made with love and traditional recipes passed down from gogo.

Mission

To provide affordable, delicious, and healthy traditional meals that remind people of home.

Vision

To become Durban's #1 trusted home-cooked meal service.
